# Warranty and repairs

## What is covered
All products carry a 12 month warranty from the delivery date, covering
manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use. Batteries are
covered for 6 months, as they are consumable.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, and damage from using a
charger or accessory we did not supply. Repairs carried out by anyone other
than us void the remainder of the warranty.

## Making a claim
We need the order number and a photo of the fault. Claims are assessed within
2 working days. Approved claims are repaired or replaced; if neither is
possible within 14 days, the item is refunded in full.

## Out of warranty
Repairs outside the warranty are quoted before any work starts. The minimum
charge is £35 including return postage.
