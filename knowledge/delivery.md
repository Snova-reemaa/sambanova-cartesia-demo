# Delivery

## Standard options
Standard delivery is 3 to 5 working days and is free over £40, otherwise £3.95.
Express delivery is next working day if ordered before 4pm, at £6.95.
Saturday delivery is £9.95 and must be ordered before 4pm on Friday.

## When an order is late
An order counts as late once it is 2 working days past the latest date on the
confirmation email. Late orders are refunded the delivery charge automatically.
If an order is more than 10 working days late it is treated as lost, and the
customer chooses a replacement or a full refund.

## Tracking
Tracking goes live once the parcel is scanned at the depot, usually the evening
of dispatch. A tracking number that shows nothing for more than 24 hours after
dispatch means the parcel was not scanned and should be reported.

## Where we deliver
UK mainland, Northern Ireland, Isle of Man and the Channel Islands. We do not
currently ship outside the UK.
