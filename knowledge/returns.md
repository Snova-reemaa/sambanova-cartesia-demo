# Returns and refunds

## Standard return window
Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened items can be returned within 14 days, and are refunded at 80% of the
purchase price unless the item is faulty.

## Faulty items
A faulty item is refunded in full at any point within the 12 month warranty,
opened or not. We cover return postage for faulty items. The customer covers
return postage in every other case.

## Items we cannot accept back
Headphones, earphones and earbuds once the packaging has been opened —
opening the box breaks the hygiene seal, so opened headphones cannot be
returned at all unless they are faulty. Software with an
activated licence key. Gift cards. Anything bought from a third-party reseller
rather than from us directly.

## How long a refund takes
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the
item arriving back at the warehouse. Card refunds then take a further 3 to 5
working days to appear, depending on the bank.
